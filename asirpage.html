How I Work with Overseas Buyers

Buying property in Lahore from abroad shouldn’t require blind trust in a relative’s opinion or a glossy sales pitch. Here is my exact 6-step process from our first call to handover.

Step 01 · Free Consultation

Discovery Call

We discuss your budget, investment goal (rental yield, capital growth, or future family home), and comfortable installment horizon.

Step 02 · Side-by-Side Comparison

Honest Shortlist

I narrow the market to 2–3 verified projects and show you their advantages, risks, payment plans, and expected timelines side by side.

Step 03 · Due Diligence

Verification Before Payment

Before you transfer a single rupee, we verify authority approvals (LDA/CBD), developer delivery history, and exact unit allotment rules.

Step 04 · Safe Banking

Booking & Payment Guidance

Step-by-step guidance on official Roshan Digital Account (RDA) or direct developer banking channels so every payment has an official trail.

Step 05 · On-Ground Eyes

Updates Until Handover

Periodic site progress updates, ledger confirmations, and milestone tracking so you always know what is happening on the ground.

Step 06 · Long-Term Support

After the Sale

Support with possession, rental management coordination, or future resale exit when you decide to realize your returns.
