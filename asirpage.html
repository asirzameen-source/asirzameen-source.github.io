Buying from the UK, UAE or US

You can’t visit every site, so you need someone who handles the details on the ground. This is how I work with overseas buyers:

  1. 1
    We talk first. Your budget, goal (live in, rent out or resell) and timeline.
  2. 2
    I shortlist honestly. Two or three projects with the pros, cons and payment plans side by side.
  3. 3
    You verify before you pay. I walk you through the documents and what to check.
  4. 4
    I stay with you after booking. Updates and answers until you have the file in hand.
